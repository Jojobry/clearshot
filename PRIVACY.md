# ClearShot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-02_

ClearShot is built to be private by default.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** ClearShot does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any
personal data or browsing information.

## How it works

- Screenshots are captured and stitched entirely inside your browser.
- The captured image is passed to the editor tab using your browser's local
  storage and is deleted as soon as the editor loads it.
- The final image only goes where you send it: your clipboard, or a PNG file you
  download to your own computer.

## Network

ClearShot makes **no network requests**. There is no server, no account, no
login, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

## Permissions

- **activeTab** — lets ClearShot capture the current tab, only at the moment you
  click the icon or press the shortcut.
- **scripting** — injects the script that scrolls and measures the page so the
  full page can be captured.
- **storage / unlimitedStorage** — temporarily holds the captured image so the
  editor tab can open it; removed immediately afterward.
- **clipboardWrite** — used only by the "Copy" button.
